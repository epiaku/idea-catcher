### Why This Setup Works Well for YouTube

1. **Standard 16:9 Aspect Ratio (No Black Bars):**
	- If you tried to record the full 32:9 ultrawide screen (
		$$
		5120×1440
		$$
		), YouTube would add massive black bars above and below your video on normal screens.
		- Because PbP splits the screen into a standard 
		$$
		2560×1440
		$$
		 area, your captured Mac feed fills the entire YouTube player cleanly.
2. **Crisp Code & Text Legibility:**
	- At 1440p, code syntax in VS Code, terminal windows, and browser text remain sharp and easy for viewers to read, even when downsampled or compressed.
