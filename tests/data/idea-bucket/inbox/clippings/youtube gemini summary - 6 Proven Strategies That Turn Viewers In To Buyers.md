To guide entrepreneurs, creators, and business owners in selecting and implementing the right sales funnel for their price points and offers, leveraging YouTube content to cross the "trust line" and convert viewers into customers efficiently.

### Key Examples

- **$2,000 Direct Sales Offer:** Selling a $2,000 product directly via YouTube descriptions ("Buy Now" funnel), illustrating that simple funnels can work even for higher prices, though under $250 is recommended.

- **$40,000 Ascension Ladder:** Converting buyers from a $99 initial entry product up to a $40,000 flagship service through automated post-purchase upsells.

### Action Plan

- **Understand the Trust Line:** Measure the price of your offer against the amount of trust required; higher-priced offers require more touchpoints before buyers complete a purchase.
- **Select the Appropriate Funnel:**
	- Use the **Buy Now Funnel** for low-ticket offers under $250 (digital products, apps, software, communities).
		- Use the **Call Funnel** for high-ticket offers ($3,000–$10,000+) requiring personalized trust and qualification.
		- Use the **Waitlist Funnel** for cohort programs, limited availability, or creating fixed-window launch urgency.
		- Use the **DM Funnel** when your target audience prefers direct, low-pressure text messaging (Instagram/WhatsApp) over sales calls.
		- Use the **Webinar Funnel** once per quarter to educate, remove objections, and sell offers across any price range.
		- Use the **Ascension Funnel** only after you have one proven offer and want to automate structured, post-purchase upsells.
- **Qualify Prospects Wisely:** On sales call forms, keep friction manageable—review application details manually or send simple follow-ups rather than using rigid auto-rejection logic.
- **Implement Rigorous Follow-Up Sequences:** Consistently follow up via automated email sequences post-call or post-webinar, as 30% to 40% of sales occur during follow-up interactions.
